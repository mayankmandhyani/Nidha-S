Our Story
"We didn't want a brand that photographs well and wears badly. So we built the other kind."

Nidha's began at a small cutting table and a stubborn belief: that Indian ethnic fabric could be chosen as carefully as the garment it becomes. Every design is checked by hand, cut in small batches rather than mass runs, and sold complete — as the full set it was designed in, not split apart.

Nidha Founder & Head of Design
