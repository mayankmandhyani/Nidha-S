Our Story
"We didn't want a brand that photographs well and wears badly. So we built the other kind."

Nidha's began as a small tailoring table and a stubborn belief: that Indian ethnic wear could be both properly made and properly wearable. Every fabric is chosen before the design is, every Chikankari panel is checked by hand, and every set is cut in small batches rather than mass runs — so what you see is what actually arrives.

Nidha Founder & Head of Design
